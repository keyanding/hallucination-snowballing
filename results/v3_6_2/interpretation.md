# Interpretation

**ROBUST_CONTEXT_ASSAY** under the frozen, user-amended hierarchy.

All four context sizes produced C0=40/40, W0=40/40 and paired state-following=40/40. No mapped call produced UNKNOWN, OTHER or INVALID. Gold, wrong-state and paired degradation relative to K0 were all 0/40; there were no discordant cases in either direction. The monotonic-collapse condition was false.

At K24, the 12 seeded cases were correct in both arms at beginning, middle and end: 24/24 calls per position. All 24 exact primary/secondary prompt repeats were token-identical. No position-related error was observed in this subset. This is evidence for the tested positions and cases, not proof of universal position invariance.

The experiment did not find a failure boundary within 0–24 irrelevant mappings. It supports the licensed conclusion that the minimal supplied-state mapping primitive remains highly reliable under this degree of synthetic context accumulation. It does not establish reliability for substantially longer contexts, conflicting evidence, an upstream entity/reasoning layer or semantically similar real-world distractors.

Each 40/40 proportion has Wilson 95% interval approximately [91.2%, 100%]. Perfect observed performance is not a guarantee of population-level perfection. The primary statistical unit is 40 paired base cases, not 320 independent populations; repeated positions also share their 12 base cases.

The mapping blocks are nested by insertion only. Relevant pair order and relative beginning/middle/end region remain fixed within each case at positive K, balanced 14/13/13 across cases. K0 has only two lines, so its three-region interpretation is inapplicable; gold-first and wrong-first are balanced 20/20. Adding context changes absolute distances, and the design does not identify an internal retrieval mechanism.

The results do not establish natural hallucination, multi-hop snowballing, SHAR/HalluSE effects or mechanistic explanations. No follow-up stage was executed.
